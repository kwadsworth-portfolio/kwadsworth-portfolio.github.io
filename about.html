About Me

My project management background provides strong transferable skills directly relevant to cybersecurity and GRC roles — including analytical problem-solving, risk-based decision-making, documentation, stakeholder communication and structured delivery.

This portfolio demonstrates my transition into cybersecurity and my commitment to building strong technical foundations while applying established professional experience. I am targeting roles such as GRC Analyst, GRC Manager, Cyber Project or Programme Manager, and Information Security Management positions.
